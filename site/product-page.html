Product page

The product page is built from blocks. Open a product in the theme editor, then drag blocks to reorder them or hide the ones you don't need.

Main blocks

Title, tagline and price

  • Title can color its last word with the product color.
  • Tagline uses your text, or the product's tagline field.
  • Price can show a "per serving" price. It reads the number at the start of the variant name, like "30 servings".

Flavor links

Round buttons that link to your other flavors. Pick the products in the block, or fill the custom.siblings field. See fields →

Variant picker

Buttons for size, flavor and other options. Turn on Show swatches to show colors or images when your options have them.

Subscribe & save

Shows one-time and subscription choices. It needs a subscription app, like Shopify Subscriptions. No app means the block stays hidden.

Bundle tiers

Cards like "1 pouch / 2 pouches, save 10% / 3 pouches, save 15%". Picking one sets the quantity.

Important: the "save" numbers are just labels. To give the real discount, create matching automatic discounts in Discounts → Amount off products with a minimum quantity.

Buy buttons

Add to cart, plus optional: quantity box, Buy it now buttons, installment info, store pickup and a short note.

Extra blocks

  • Read the label: a supplement or nutrition facts panel. Add an image of your label, or type the rows.
  • Benefits: up to 4 short points with check marks.
  • Trust badges: 3 small icons, like "Free shipping" or "Lab tested".
  • Collapsible row: for ingredients, how to use and shipping info.
  • Star rating: needs a review app.
  • Disclaimer, Small line and Custom Liquid for anything else.
  • Apps: app blocks can go anywhere in the list.

Section settings

  • Sticky add to cart bar: shows at the bottom once the main button scrolls away.
  • Accent color: overrides the product color for this template only.
