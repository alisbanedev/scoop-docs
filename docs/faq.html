FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

My products all show the same color.

Each product needs its own Accent color field. See Add your products.

The browser tab or checkout shows the wrong store name.

Change it in Settings → General → Store name. The theme can't change checkout.

Subscribe & save doesn't show.

It needs a subscription app, like Shopify Subscriptions, and the product must be added to a plan in that app.

Bundle tiers show "save 10%" but the cart has no discount.

The labels don't create discounts. Add automatic discounts with a minimum quantity in Discounts. Make them match the labels.

Star ratings don't show.

Install a review app that fills Shopify's standard rating fields. Most popular review apps do.

How do I add an app's widget?

In the theme editor, click Add block (product page) or Add section → Apps. Your app's blocks are listed there.

Can I force dark mode?

Yes. Theme settings → Colors → Color scheme → Dark.

Does Scoop support more than one language or currency?

Yes. Turn on the country and language selectors in the Header or Footer. Add languages and markets in Shopify settings first.

Will theme updates erase my changes?

No, as long as you change things in the theme editor. Code edits can be lost, so keep a copy of the theme before you update.
