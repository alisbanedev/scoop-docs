Start here

Scoop is a bold Shopify theme for brands with a few hero products, like supplements, drinks and snacks. Each product gets its own color, and the whole page follows it.

Get live in 5 steps

  1. Add the theme. Shopify admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme.
  2. Set your brand. Pick your colors, fonts and logo. How →
  3. Give each product a color. This is what makes Scoop special. How →
  4. Build your home page. Add, remove and reorder sections. How →
  5. Check your product page. Turn blocks on or off. How →
