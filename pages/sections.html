Sections

Sections are the big pieces of a page. In the theme editor, click Add section to add one, then drag to reorder.

Top of the page

Product showcase

A big rotating hero. Add one block per product or flavor. The background takes each product's color.

Image banner

One big image with a heading and a button. Set the focal point on the image in Content → Files so the important part never gets cropped.

Announcement bar

A thin bar at the very top. It can show a free shipping progress message.

Products

Product grid

Colored product cards. You can add a dark promo card with tags and a button. The big word behind each product can show always, on hover, or never. You can also pick its position, strength and outline style.

Featured product

One product with its photos, videos, price, options and buy buttons, on any page. Supports app blocks and Custom Liquid.

Collection list

Tiles that link to your collections.

Featured collection

Shows products from one collection, with an optional "View all" button.

Product recommendations

"You may also like." Shopify picks related products for you.

Tell your story

Bento

A grid of stat cards (like "5g per scoop") and photo cards.

Listicle

"5 reasons to try us." Each reason has an image, a heading and text.

Comparison

You vs. the others, row by row.

Sticker stack

A wide photo with colorful tag stickers on top.

Rich text

A heading, text and a button. Good for a short brand story.

Image with text

A photo next to a heading, text and a button.

Video

Upload a video or paste a YouTube or Vimeo link.

Testimonials

Customer quotes with stars, a name and an optional photo.

Logo list

"As seen in" logos. Each can link somewhere.

FAQ

Questions and answers. Shows a few first, with a "Show more" button.

Bottom of the page

Email signup

Collects emails into your customer list with the "newsletter" tag.

Closing CTA

A final push to buy. Shows up to 3 products in a fan, with flavor links.

Utility

  • Contact form: a simple form that sends to your store email.
  • Apps: a spot for app blocks.
  • Custom Liquid: for code you or your developer add.

Color mode: every section has a Color mode setting. Pick Light or Dark to make one section stand out, or keep "Same as theme".

Tip: most sections pick up the product color automatically. Use the "Accent color" setting only to override it.
