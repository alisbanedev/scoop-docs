Set up your store

Do these once. They take about 15 minutes.

1. Add the theme

  1. Go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. Add Scoop to your theme library.
  3. Click Customize to open the theme editor.
  4. When you're happy, click Publish.

2. Brand basics

In the theme editor, open Theme settings (the gear icon).

  • Colors: pick light, dark or auto. Then set the button color and the highlight color.
  • Typography: pick a heading font and a body font.
  • Layout: brand name, page width, corner roundness and favicon.

Add your logo in the Header section. No logo yet? The header shows your logo text instead.

3. Store name and search info

These come from Shopify, not from the theme.

  • Store name: Settings → General. It shows in the browser tab and at checkout.
  • Home page title and description: Online Store → Preferences. Google uses these.
  • Checkout look: Settings → Checkout → Customize. Add your logo and colors.

4. Menus

Go to Online Store → Navigation.

  • Use the main menu for the header.
  • Make 2 small menus for the footer, like "Shop" and "Help".
  • Pick them in the Header and Footer sections.

5. Social links

Paste your profile links in Theme settings → Social media. Icons appear in the footer. Empty ones stay hidden.
