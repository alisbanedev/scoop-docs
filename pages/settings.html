Theme settings

Theme settings change the whole store. Open the theme editor and click the gear icon.

Colors

  • Color scheme: Auto, Dark or Light. Auto follows each shopper's device.
  • Light and dark colors: background, card, text and muted text, for each mode.
  • Button: button background and text.
  • Highlight: the fallback product color. Used for accent words and badges.
  • Sale price: the color of sale prices.

Typography

Pick a heading font and a body font. Bold, condensed fonts suit Scoop's headings best.

Layout

  • Brand name: shown when you have no logo image. Defaults to your store name.
  • Page width and corner radius.
  • Favicon: the small icon in the browser tab. Use a square image, 64×64 or bigger.

Cart

Free shipping goal: the order total that gets free shipping, in your store currency. Shoppers see how close they are in the announcement bar and cart. Set it to 0 to turn it off.

This is only a message. Set the real free shipping rate in Settings → Shipping and delivery.

Social media

Paste your full profile links. Icons show in the footer.
